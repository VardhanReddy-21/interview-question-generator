JavaScript

Microtask vs Macrotask

Click card to reveal answer...

Microtasks: Promise callbacks (.then), queueMicrotask. Drains completely before the next macrotask!

Macrotasks: setTimeout, setInterval, I/O. Executed one per loop cycle.

Python

CPython GIL

Click card to reveal answer...

GIL: Mutex preventing multiple threads from executing bytecode simultaneously, protecting reference counters.

Use multiprocessing for CPU-bound tasks and asyncio for I/O tasks.

Databases

🚀 4 Principles to Ace Big Tech & Startup Interviews

1. Structure Over Speed:

Never rush into an answer immediately. Take 5–10 seconds to outline your thoughts. State your main thesis first, then elaborate with technical mechanisms.

2. The STAR+R Framework:

For behavioral questions, always anchor with Situation, Task, Action, Result, and Reflection (what you learned or would do differently).

3. Concrete Metrics Win:

Instead of saying "I optimized the database", say "I added indexes and connection pooling, reducing query response times by 40% under peak load".

4. Ask Strategic Questions:

When the interviewer asks "Do you have any questions for us?", ask about engineering trade-offs, deployment frequencies, and technical debt priorities.
